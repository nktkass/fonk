🪂 How airdrops are paid

At the end of each cycle, fonk marks the realized profit of the fee-funded book. 80% of that profit is converted and airdropped to holders pro-rata by holdings. The remaining 20% compounds into the treasury so the book — and future airdrops — grow over time.

🗓️ Airdrop cycle

Cycles settle on a fixed cadence. A live countdown and the queued airdrop amount are shown in the terminal. Airdrops are non-custodial — they settle directly to your wallet and never pass through a third party.

Fee FAQ

Does fonk keep any of my fee?
No. 100% of your fee becomes trading capital. fonk only ever grows from a share of the profit it generates, not from the fee itself.
What if the book loses money in a cycle?
If a cycle is unprofitable there is no profit to airdrop, so no airdrop is paid that cycle. Risk caps and a treasury floor are designed to limit drawdowns, but losses are possible.
Are there management or subscription fees?
None. There is no fee for holding, no fee to withdraw, and no subscription. The only fee is the flat per-transaction fee.
How is my airdrop amount decided?
Airdrops are distributed pro-rata to your share of total holdings at the cycle snapshot. Hold more, receive a larger slice of the profit pool.
Where can I see the trades?
The live terminal on the home page streams fonk's order flow, open positions and portfolio P&L in real time.
